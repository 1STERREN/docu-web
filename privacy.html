Privacy Policy for Docu

Effective & Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Welcome to Docu ("we," "our," or "the App"). Docu is an offline-first personal finance management, asset tracking (stocks & gold), and cashflow planning application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal and financial information. This Privacy Policy details our data handling practices and our explicit compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy.

1. Information We Collect and Process

A. Local Device Storage (Offline-First)

  • Financial Records: Transaction notes, amounts, categories, budget limits, tax calculations, and receipt metadata saved in the local SQLite database.
  • Asset Portfolio: Investment entries, wallets, and stock/gold holding numbers.
  • All primary financial calculations occur directly on your device. We do not operate external central databases to collect or store your private logs.

B. Data Accessed via Google APIs

When you link your Google Account to enable synchronization and backup features:

  • Google Profile Identity: Basic account email and display name strictly to verify connection state and display linked user info.
  • Google Sheets API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets):
    Used to create/update your personal Docu_Database spreadsheet, sync transaction tables, and utilize temporary formula scratchpads (Stocks_database) for live asset pricing via GOOGLEFINANCE.
  • Google Drive API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive):
    Used to maintain a dedicated Docu_Backups folder in your personal Google Drive for uploading and restoring compressed database snapshots (docu_backup_*.zip).

2. Purpose & Utilization of Google User Data

Google user data is used exclusively to facilitate explicit, user-initiated actions:

  1. Private Cloud Backup & Restore: Storing SQLite snapshots into your own personal Google Drive storage.
  2. Spreadsheet Synchronization: Exporting financial records and transaction logs to your Google Sheets file.
  3. Market Data Lookups: Querying public market values for stocks and precious metals directly within your personal spreadsheet.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
Docu's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Under these requirements:

  • We DO NOT use Google user data to train, retrain, or improve generalized Artificial Intelligence (AI) or Machine Learning (ML) models.
  • We DO NOT sell, rent, or transfer your Google data or financial logs to advertising networks or data brokers.
  • We DO NOT use your Google Sheets or Drive content for personalized advertising.
  • Humans will not read your Google user data unless you give explicit permission for support, it is necessary for security analysis, or required by law.

3. Data Security & Storage

  • Tokens: Google OAuth tokens are stored in private, sandboxed app storage and used solely to communicate with official Google endpoints.
  • Transmission: All API communication uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • Passwords: Docu never requests or stores your Google account password.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

  • Disconnect: You can disconnect your Google account anytime via the App's sync settings.
  • Revoke Remote Access: You can revoke Docu's authorization anytime via Google Account Permissions.
  • Direct Deletion: Because all files reside in your personal Google Drive and device, you can delete the Docu_Backups folder, Docu_Database spreadsheet, or clear app local data at any time.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding Docu's privacy practices:

  • Email: thedesignervr@gmail.com
  • App: Docu - Daily Organized Cashflow Utility

Kebijakan Privasi Docu

Berlaku & Terakhir Diperbarui: 7 Oktober 2026

Selamat datang di Docu ("kami" atau "Aplikasi"). Docu adalah aplikasi pencatatan keuangan pribadi, pelacak portofolio aset (saham & emas), serta kalkulator cashflow dengan prinsip offline-first.

Kami menghargai privasi Anda dan berkomitmen menjaga keamanan data keuangan Anda. Kebijakan ini menjelaskan bagaimana data dikelola serta kepatuhan kami terhadap Google API Services User Data Policy.

1. Data yang Dikumpulkan dan Diproses

A. Penyimpanan Lokal di Perangkat (Offline-First)

  • Catatan Transaksi: Nominal, kategori, catatan, batas anggaran, kalkulasi pajak, dan foto bukti struk yang tersimpan di database lokal SQLite.
  • Portofolio Aset: Catatan tabungan, dompet, dan kuantitas investasi saham/emas.
  • Seluruh data utama tersimpan di memori perangkat Anda. Kami tidak memiliki server terpusat untuk mengumpulkan riwayat keuangan Anda.

B. Data yang Diakses melalui Google API

Saat Anda menghubungkan akun Google untuk fitur sinkronisasi dan cadangan:

  • Profil Google: Nama dan alamat email untuk memverifikasi akun aktif.

    Membuat dan memperbarui spreadsheet Docu_Database untuk ekspor catatan, serta lembar kerja sementara (Stocks_database) untuk membaca harga saham & emas via formula GOOGLEFINANCE.
  • Google Drive API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive):
    Membuat folder Docu_Backups di Google Drive pribadi Anda untuk mencadangkan dan memulihkan snapshot database SQLite (docu_backup_*.zip).

2. Kepatuhan Ketentuan Google (Limited Use Disclosure)

Penggunaan data yang diperoleh Docu melalui Google API tunduk sepenuhnya pada Google API Services User Data Policy, termasuk ketentuan Limited Use.

  • Kami TIDAK menjual atau membagikan data Google maupun data keuangan Anda ke pihak ketiga atau pengiklan.
  • Data tidak digunakan untuk personalisasi iklan.
  • Tidak ada staf manusia yang membaca isi spreadsheet atau cadangan Drive Anda kecuali atas izin eksplisit Anda saat bantuan teknis.

3. Keamanan Data & Hak Pengguna
